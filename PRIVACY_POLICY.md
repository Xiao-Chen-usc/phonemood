# Privacy Policy for PhoneMood

**Effective date: September 6, 2026**

PhoneMood ("the App") is a local-only Android application designed to help users observe the relationship between their phone usage and self-reported mood. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how that information is used, and how users can control it.

## 1. Information PhoneMood Accesses

PhoneMood may access or create the following information on your device:

- **App usage information:** information made available through Android Usage Access, such as which apps are used, package names, usage timestamps, and usage duration.
- **Mood ratings:** mood scores and responses that you voluntarily enter in the App.
- **App-generated records:** local summaries, timelines, session information, and analysis derived from your usage information and mood ratings.
- **Exported reports:** if you choose to export your data, PhoneMood can create a JSON report containing information such as app package names, usage durations, timestamps, and mood ratings.

PhoneMood does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, messages, or payment information.

## 2. How Information Is Used

The information described above is used only to provide PhoneMood's on-device features, including:

- calculating and displaying phone and app usage time;
- prompting you to record a mood rating after configurable usage intervals;
- displaying timelines, summaries, and period analysis;
- generating daily or period reports; and
- exporting your data when you request an export.

PhoneMood is intended as a self-observation tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical or psychological diagnoses.

## 3. Local Processing and Data Storage

PhoneMood is designed to operate locally on your Android device.

- The App does **not** require an account.
- The App does **not** use a PhoneMood server or cloud backend.
- The App does **not** include advertising SDKs or analytics/telemetry SDKs.
- The App does **not** request the Android `INTERNET` permission.
- Usage records, mood ratings, and generated analysis are stored locally on your device.

Because PhoneMood does not transmit this information to the developer, the developer does not receive, store, or have access to your PhoneMood usage records or mood ratings.

## 4. Permissions

Depending on your Android version and the features you enable, PhoneMood may request the following permissions or system access:

### Usage Access

PhoneMood uses Android Usage Access to determine active phone and app usage. This access is necessary for its core usage-monitoring functionality. Usage information is processed locally on your device.

### Notifications

PhoneMood may request permission to send notifications so it can remind you to complete a mood check-in or inform you about monitoring status.

### Display Over Other Apps

If you enable floating mood check-ins, PhoneMood may request permission to display over other apps. This permission is used only to show the PhoneMood check-in interface and does not allow PhoneMood to read the contents of other apps.

You can revoke these permissions at any time through Android system settings, although doing so may disable related PhoneMood features.

## 5. Data Sharing

PhoneMood does not sell, rent, or share your locally stored PhoneMood data with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

PhoneMood does not automatically upload your usage records, mood ratings, or reports to third parties.

If you choose to export or share a PhoneMood report, you control where that file is sent. Once you share an exported file with another app, service, person, or AI system, that recipient's privacy practices apply. You should review the recipient and the contents of the report before sharing it.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

PhoneMood data remains on your device until it is deleted through App functionality, Android system controls, clearing the App's storage, or uninstalling the App, as applicable.

Exported report files are separate files under your control and may remain on your device after App data is cleared or the App is uninstalled. You can delete exported reports using your device's file-management tools.

Because PhoneMood does not maintain a remote user account or server-side database, there is no remote PhoneMood account data for the developer to delete.

## 7. Security

PhoneMood minimizes privacy risk by keeping its core data processing and storage on the device and by not transmitting PhoneMood records to a developer-operated server.

However, no device or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate security on their devices and for choosing carefully where exported reports are shared.

## 8. Children's Privacy

PhoneMood is not specifically directed to children. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated server because PhoneMood does not operate such a server or account system.

## 9. Third-Party Services

PhoneMood's core functionality does not rely on advertising, analytics, or developer-operated cloud services that receive your usage records or mood ratings.

If you independently choose to share an exported PhoneMood report with a third-party application or service, that third party's terms and privacy policy govern its handling of the information you provide to it.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if PhoneMood's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. Material changes will be reflected by updating this document and its effective date.
